# Read the dashboard

The Ralphdex dashboard is your single view of what happened and why.

- **First-Run Readiness** — workspace, tasks, provider, doctrine, and validation status at a glance.
- **Current work** — the selected task, iteration count, and the latest outcome.
- **Stop reason** — a plain-language explanation of why the loop stopped and what to do next.
- **Diagnostics & artifacts** — prompt evidence, provenance bundles, and failure analysis for any iteration.

Open it any time with **Show Status**, or use the Ralphdex activity-bar icon for the compact sidebar view.
